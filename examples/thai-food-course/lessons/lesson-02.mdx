# บทที่ 2 (Lesson 2) — Tastes & Flavors

## Introduction

Thai cuisine is famous for balancing multiple flavors in a single dish. Understanding taste vocabulary lets you describe what you want, ask for adjustments, and appreciate the complexity of Thai cooking.

## The Five Fundamental Tastes

:::vocabulary-set{id="thai-tastes" title="The Five Tastes"}

::vocab{id="phet" word="เผ็ด" transliteration="phèt" translation="spicy" partOfSpeech="adjective"}

::vocab{id="waan" word="หวาน" transliteration="wǎan" translation="sweet" partOfSpeech="adjective"}

::vocab{id="priao" word="เปรี้ยว" transliteration="prîao" translation="sour" partOfSpeech="adjective"}

::vocab{id="khem" word="เค็ม" transliteration="khem" translation="salty" partOfSpeech="adjective"}

::vocab{id="khom" word="ขม" transliteration="khǒm" translation="bitter" partOfSpeech="adjective"}

:::

### Taste Balance

Great Thai dishes balance these tastes. For example:
- **ต้มยำ** (tom yam): sour + spicy + salty
- **ผัดไทย** (pad thai): sweet + sour + salty
- **แกงเขียวหวาน** (green curry): sweet + spicy + salty

## Exercises

:::exercise{id="ex-taste-01" type="fill-in-blank" difficulty="beginner"}

**Question:** Complete the sentence: อาหารไทยมีรส _____ (Thai food is spicy)

**Answer:** เผ็ด

**Explanation:** เผ็ด means spicy, one of the five fundamental tastes.

:::

:::exercise{id="ex-taste-02" type="translation" difficulty="beginner"}

**Question:** Translate: "I want it a little spicy"

**Answer:** เอาเผ็ดนิดหน่อย

:::

## Key Points

1. **Five tastes**: เผ็ด, หวาน, เปรี้ยว, เค็ม, ขม
2. **Spice adjustment**: Use ไม่เผ็ด, เผ็ดนิดหน่อย, เผ็ดมาก
3. **Balance**: Great Thai food balances multiple tastes
4. **Preferences**: Use ชอบ (like) and ไม่ชอบ (don't like)
