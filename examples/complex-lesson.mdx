# Advanced Grammar Patterns

## Grammar

:::grammar-rule{id="conditional-pattern" title="Conditional Sentences"}

Conditional sentences express hypothetical situations and their consequences.

### Structure

- **If-clause** (condition)
- **Main clause** (result)

### Types

1. **Real conditionals** - Possible situations
2. **Unreal conditionals** - Hypothetical situations

:::example{id="ex-cond-01" text="If it rains, we will stay home." translation="अगर बारिश होगी, तो हम घर रहेंगे।" illustrates="conditional-pattern"}
Real conditional - possible future event.
:::

:::example{id="ex-cond-02" text="If I had studied, I would have passed." translation="अगर मैंने पढ़ाई की होती, तो मैं पास हो जाता।" illustrates="conditional-pattern"}
Unreal conditional - past hypothetical.
:::

:::

## Vocabulary

:::vocabulary-set{id="vocab-advanced" title="Advanced Vocabulary"}

::vocab{id="vocab-hypothetical" word="hypothetical" translation="काल्पनिक" partOfSpeech="adjective"}
Based on assumption rather than fact.
::

::vocab{id="vocab-consequence" word="consequence" translation="परिणाम" partOfSpeech="noun"}
A result or effect of an action.
::

::vocab{id="vocab-conditional" word="conditional" translation="सशर्त" partOfSpeech="adjective"}
Subject to one or more conditions.
::

:::

## Exercises

:::exercise{id="ex-advanced-01" type="transformation" difficulty="advanced"}

**Instructions:** Transform the following sentences into conditional form.

1. I study hard. I pass the exam.
2. She doesn't practice. She doesn't improve.
3. They arrive early. They get good seats.

**Answers:**
1. If I study hard, I will pass the exam.
2. If she doesn't practice, she won't improve.
3. If they arrive early, they will get good seats.

**Explanation:** These are all real conditionals expressing possible future outcomes.

:::

:::exercise{id="ex-advanced-02" type="multiple-choice" difficulty="advanced"}

**Question:** Which sentence is an unreal conditional?

A) If it rains, I will bring an umbrella.
B) If I were rich, I would travel the world.
C) If you study, you will learn.

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Option B uses "were" and "would", indicating a hypothetical situation that is not currently true.

:::
